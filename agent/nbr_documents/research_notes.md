# Bangladesh NBR Tax Research — FY2026‑27 (1 July 2026 – 30 June 2027)
**Prepared for:** project owner, supply‑portal / ERP accounts‑payable system
**Research date:** 25 August 2026 (≈2 months into FY2026‑27)
**Bottom line:** the two operative FY2026‑27 instruments were located and read in full — the Withholding Tax Rules 2026 (income tax TDS) and the VDS Rules 2025 as amended for FY2026‑27 (withholding VAT). Eight income‑tax figures survived independent verification. Everything else in this report is **single‑source and must be re‑read against the official PDF before it drives a payment.**

## 3. Unverified / conflicting figures

> **Do not load anything in this section into the production rate tables without a human reading the official PDF page cited.** Every figure here was read once, by one researcher, from a Bangla PDF that required page rendering. They are probably right. "Probably" is not good enough for a payment engine.

### 3a. The two‑SRO trap — the single most dangerous finding

Two different SROs are **both titled উৎসে কর বিধিমালা, ২০২৬** and both claim effect from 1 July 2026:

- **S.R.O. 210‑Ain/Aykar‑1/2026**, 8 June 2026
- **S.R.O. 273‑Ain/Aykar‑5/2026**, gazetted 5 July 2026 — NBR labels this one "(সংশোধিত)"

Their **s.89 goods tables are identical** (SRO 273 only adds "আয়োডিন" to serial 4). Their **s.90 services tables materially differ**:

| Services item | SRO 210 (June) | SRO 273 (July — governs) |
|---|---|---|
| Serial 5 basket (catering, PR, event mgmt, training, courier, packing & shifting, collection agency) | **4%** of total bill | **2%** of total bill |
| ক্রিয়েটিভ মিডিয়া + প্রিন্ট ও ইলেকট্রনিক মিডিয়া এজেন্সি | inside serial 5 | moved to **serial 4** (10% commission / 1% total bill) |
| Proviso (খ) — higher‑of commission vs gross bill, serials 4/12/13/18 | **absent** | **present** |

**Anyone who ingested the June PDF has wrong service rates today.** Any third‑party chart, consultant spreadsheet, or ERP config dated June 2026 must be re‑checked against SRO 273.

## 4. Gaps — what could not be obtained, and why

**1. The FY2026‑27 Income Tax Paripatra does not exist yet.** This is a *confirmed negative*, not a search failure. NBR's paripatra index was read in both English and Bangla (https://nbr.gov.bd/taxtypes/income-tax/income-tax-paripatra/eng and `/ban`); the newest entry is আয়কর পরিপত্র, ২০২৫‑২০২৬, then 2024‑25, 2023‑24, and older. The Income Tax publications index likewise stops at *Income Tax Nirdeshika (2025‑2026)*, dated 12/08/2025. This matches NBR's normal cadence — the FY2025‑26 paripatra was published on 28 August 2025, ~2 months after that budget. **Re‑check in early‑to‑mid September 2026.** Its absence does **not** create a rate gap: the paripatra is explanatory; the binding rates live in the Finance Act and the Withholding Tax Rules SRO, both of which were obtained.

**2. No standalone FY2026‑27 VDS guideline (উৎসে ভ্যাট কর্তন ও আদায় নির্দেশিকা) appears to exist.** Established by enumerating three NBR listing pages (VAT publications, both VAT SRO listings, VAT general orders) — not by a single search. NBR published a 47‑page *VAT Instruction (2026‑27)* officer circular, but no fiscal‑year VDS handbook. **Conclusion: the binding FY2026‑27 VDS text is the VDS Rules 2025 + SRO 140/2026. Do not wait for a handbook.**

**3. No consolidated FY2026‑27 VDS rules text exists.** NBR published only an **amending** SRO. The 45‑serial table in this research is a hand‑merge. Every individual rate and code is quoted from a primary source, but **the merged table has never been read as a single official document anywhere.**

**4. bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd — inconsistent reachability, and this cost the research dearly.** All four research passes reported total failure: `connect ECONNREFUSED 114.130.118.50:443`, `read ECONNRESET`, and certificate‑chain errors, across http and https, act‑1106 (VAT Bangla), act‑1088/1148 (VAT English), act‑1409/1429 (Income Tax), and print views — 6+ attempts each. During the verification pass the host **did** respond (act‑1429/section‑51919 and the ITA 2023 PDF were both read successfully). So the site is intermittently reachable, not dead. **Everything below is blocked on it:**
   - the **current consolidated Third Schedule** of the VAT & SD Act — i.e. the per‑HS‑code / per‑service‑code map to 5% / 7.5% / 10% / fixed‑amount. Without this, no specific supplier line's reduced VAT rate can be looked up.
   - the Third Schedule's operative paragraphs (অনুচ্ছেদ ১–৩), which govern *how* the table rates apply.
   - the full **First Schedule** exempt list.
   - the **current wording of VAT Act ss. 2(21), 46, 49, 51, 53** — see the withholding‑entity conflict below.

**5. Unresolved legal conflict: is a PRIVATE limited company a VAT withholding entity?** The VDS Rules 2025 r.2(1)(খ) say "কোনো লিমিটেড কোম্পানি" — **any** limited company — and separately catch anyone with turnover over Tk 10 crore. The Act's s.2(21), in the only text obtainable (NBR's 2013 "English – Draft"), says "**a public limited company**". The Finance Act/Bill 2026 was verified **not** to amend s.2(21), but whether an earlier Finance Act (2013–2025) broadened it could not be checked because bdlaws was down. In practice NBR administers VDS through the Rules and every professional source follows the wider definition, so a private limited company **should** deduct — but **this needs a VAT practitioner's written confirmation, not this research.**

**6. The VAT chapter was read from the Finance BILL, not the enacted Act.** The official customs budget portal serves the same file (`Finance-BILL-2026-2027.pdf`) under both its "Finance Act (English)" and "Finance Act (Bangla)" links. Press reporting says the Bill passed **with 64 amendments**. So the new s.2(76ক) "trader" definition and the new s.20 (15% on imported services) are as **introduced**, and may differ as **enacted**. (The income‑tax chapter *was* read from the enacted gazette, Act No. 96 of 2026.)

**7. Amendment‑chain blind spot: the Finance Ordinance 2025.** The Authentic English Text of ITA 2023 carries footnotes only up to the Finance Act 2024. One Finance Ordinance 2025 change is documented (the new proviso to s.142(1), via the FY2025‑26 paripatra para 36). If the Ordinance quietly altered s.89 or s.140(5), it would not have been seen. **This does not affect the rates** — those come wholly from SRO 273, which was read directly.

**8. Machine‑readability is a hard blocker for automated ingestion.** Every Bangla NBR PDF here embeds fonts with no usable ToUnicode map. `pymupdf`/`pypdf` returns garbage — the Finance Act's s.89 area extracts as strings like `?2(+` and `B? 8 L MN OP`; the 2016 rules gazette yields `g~mK-6.3` for মূসক‑৬.৩. **Every Bangla quotation in this research is a human transcription from a page rendered to PNG at 130–150 dpi.** Digits, percentages, service codes (S001.10 etc.), dates and table structure extract reliably in the *Unicode‑with‑bad‑cmap* files; the *legacy Bijoy* files (Finance Act 2026, SRO 273, Finance Ordinance 2025, VATR2016, SRO‑142) yield **zero** Bengali codepoints and even the digits are unreliable. A cheap detector: regex the extracted text for `U+0980–U+09FF`; no matches means legacy Bijoy, and you must render or OCR.

**9. Big‑4 corroboration is thinner than it should be.** PwC's *Finance Act, 2026: Key Amendments* returned **HTTP 403** on repeated attempts (https://www.pwc.com/bd/en/assets/pdfs/budget/finance-act-2026-key-amendments.pdf). KPMG's fuller *Bangladesh Tax (Updated to Finance Act 2026)* was identified but not fetched. `caripon.com`, `taxvatpoint.com`, `easytaxzone.com`, `bdtaxation.com` sat behind Cloudflare interstitials or returned 403. Only the KPMG/RRH Finance **Bill** summary and one law‑firm briefing were obtained.

**10. No proof of absence for post‑July‑2026 amendments.** Today is ~2.5 months into FY2026‑27. NBR's VAT SRO listing shows nothing after 11/06/2026; the income‑tax SRO index paginates and returned only SROs 211/212/213 (all 8 June 2026, none withholding‑related). One newer NBR item — "উৎসে কর কর্তন/সংগ্রহ সংক্রান্ত অধিক্ষেত্র আদেশ (সংশোধিত)", dated 06/08/2026 — appears to concern **jurisdiction**, not rates, and was not opened. **NBR's site listings are not always current. Re‑scan before go‑live and before each fiscal‑period close.**

**11. Not investigated at all** (relevant to AP, flagged for completeness): Advance Tax (আগাম কর) rate and its FY2026‑27 treatment under SRO 139/2026; VAT registration/enlistment thresholds as enacted; the supplementary duty schedule; penalty quantum under VAT Act ss.85(1ক) and 127; and NBR's e‑service portals for BIN/PSR verification (etaxnbr.gov.bd, vat.gov.bd, etds.gov.bd, tcc.nbr.gov.bd) — all JavaScript SPAs that returned only shell titles to server‑side fetch, with **no documented public API**.

**12. Fiscal‑year vs assessment‑year trap to watch in other sources.** Chapter 1 of the FY2025‑26 paripatra is headed "২০২৬‑২০২৭ এবং ২০২৭‑২০২৮ **করবর্ষের** জন্য প্রযোজ্য আয়করের হার" — rates for **assessment** years. Assessment year 2026‑27 = income year FY2025‑26, **not** the FY2026‑27 you need. Several secondary articles conflate the two. Withholding under ss.89/90 is governed by the rules in force on the **date of payment**.

## 6. Risk note

**Nothing in this report should be treated as authoritative for computing a real supplier payment, and the reasons are structural rather than a matter of effort.** Every Bangla figure here was read by rendering a PDF page to an image and transcribing what was on screen, because NBR's gazette PDFs embed fonts with no usable Unicode mapping — the Finance Act 2026's section 89 area extracts as literal gibberish (`?2(+`, `B? 8 L MN OP`), and the 5 July gazette of the Withholding Tax Rules contains **zero Bengali codepoints** in its text layer. Transcription from rendered glyphs is careful but it is not verification, and a single mis-read digit (০.৬ vs ৬.০, ৫% vs ৭%) is a silent, systematic over- or under-deduction repeated across every bill until someone notices. Compounding this: NBR publishes **amending** SROs rather than consolidated texts, so the 45-serial VDS table this project needs **exists in no single official document anywhere** — it is a hand-merge of two SROs, and whoever signs it off is the first person to have certified it. Two different SROs share the title "উৎসে কর বিধিমালা, ২০২৬", both claim effect from 1 July 2026, and their **services** rates materially differ (4% vs 2% on catering, courier, training and events), which means a plausible, well-intentioned June-2026 download produces wrong payments today. The consolidated law repository at bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd was unreachable across four research passes and only intermittently reachable during verification, so the current wording of VAT & SD Act ss.2(21), 46, 49 and 51 — including whether your company is legally a withholding entity at all — remains genuinely unknown. Third-party charts that look authoritative are actively wrong: one publishes 7% where the gazette says 5%; several cite a VDS SRO number ("258") that does not exist. And the largest exposure is not a mis-read figure but a **silence**: whether the s.89 base is computed gross or net of VAT is not stated in section 89, section 90, section 140(5), or anywhere in the 2026 Rules, all of which were read in full — universal practice strips VAT first, but universal practice is not law, and on a Tk 100,000 bill the two treatments differ by hundreds of taka in a direction that is either under-withholding (NBR penalty, interest, and personal exposure for the officers named in VAT Act s.127) or over-withholding (supplier disputes and a payable you cannot easily recover).

**Required human sign-off before any of this drives a payment.** (1) A **Bangla-literate reader** must verify the s.89 and s.90 rate tables, line by line, against the printed SRO 273 gazette — this is a few hours of work and is the cheapest control available. (2) A **registered income-tax practitioner or CA** must confirm in writing: the VAT-exclusive-vs-inclusive base question; whether s.89 and s.97 can both fire on the agricultural basket; whether a de-minimis exists under s.90; and whether TDS remains a minimum tax in FY2026-27. (3) A **VAT practitioner** must certify the merged 45-serial VDS table as a whole, and rule on whether a private limited company is a withholding entity given the Act/Rules conflict. (4) Every rate loaded into `app/rules/fy2026-27/**.yaml` must carry a `source_doc` citation naming the SRO number, its date, and the rule and serial — and, per this project's own convention, the loader must keep rejecting any entry that lacks one. (5) The VAT-base treatment must be a **named, visible configuration flag** carrying the adviser's opinion as its source, in the same manner as the existing `po_prices_include_vat` placeholder — never a constant inside the tax engine. (6) The rate tables must stay **marked PLACEHOLDER** until steps 1–3 are complete and signed, and the CFO review UI must continue to display the deterministic breakdown with rule IDs alongside any narrative, so that a wrong rate surfaces as a visible, attributable line rather than a number nobody can trace. Finally, re-scan NBR's rules, SRO and general-order indexes before every fiscal-period close: this research cannot prove the absence of an amendment issued in the last few weeks, and NBR's own listing pages are demonstrably not current.
